NBA Power Poll — Week 6 (through Nov 30, 2025)

Records via league standings; notable results linked per team.

  1. Thunder (20–1)

    12 straight and still defending like a metronome; closed out Portland behind SGA’s 26 and Chet’s two-way floor-raising.

  2. Nuggets (14–5)

    Jokic’s weekly form remains MVP-ladder strong; machine wins minutes even when the bench shuffles.

  3. Lakers (15–4)

    Second in the West on record strength; high-usage Reaves game early set the tone while stars cycle health.

  4. Rockets (13–4)

    Three straight capped by a 129–101 in Salt Lake; Durant and Şengün toggled lead scorer roles and the defense traveled.

  5. Pistons (16–4)

    East leaders; tight, high-scoring win in Miami ended a mini-skid and kept the cadence.

  6. Spurs (13–6)

    Wemby gravity stabilizes lineups; profile stays top-8 with rim deterrence and cleaner late-game reps.

  7. Timberwolves (12–8)

    Defense + glass back near identity; offense matchup-dependent but holding the line.

  8. 76ers (10–9)

    Maxey-led creation keeps them above water through bumps; schedule-light lift coming.

  9. Knicks (13–6)

    Physicality and bench lifts push a top-3 East record; half-court structure holding.

  10. Heat (13–7)

    Lost a shootout to DET but the two-way baseline is intact; bench scoring continues to matter.

  11. Raptors (14–7)

    Barnes shoulder-load is working; secondary rim pressure unlock remains the swing item.

  12. Bucks (9–12)

    Record lagging; half-court engine still projects up once rotation settles.

  13. Cavaliers (12–9)

    Structure + late stops = high floor; trending toward solid Central footing.

  14. Suns (12–9)

    Talent obvious; closing detail and health timing decide the seed line.

  15. Warriors (11–10)

    Shot-variance week to week yet still winning enough clutch minutes to hover above water.

  16. Hawks (13–8)

    Shot quality fine; turnover profile dictates ceiling. East mid-pack with upside.

  17. Magic (12–8)

    Switchable, physical, and creeping upward as half-court timing improves.

  18. Jazz (6–13)

    Blown off their defensive spots by Houston; need healthier paint protection and glass.

  19. Grizzlies (9–12)

    Tempo control appears in pockets; closing versus elite guards remains the problem.

  20. Clippers (5–15)

    Defense shows in bursts; offense lacks consistent rim pressure. West table stakes are unforgiving.

  21. Trail Blazers (8–12)

    Competed with OKC before late separation; structure is visible even while shorthanded inside.

  22. Mavericks (6–15)

    Rookie flashes aside, interior defense and defensive rebounding cap runs.

  23. Kings (5–16)

    Offense fine; point-of-attack defense bleeding value late. Needs a stopper and a whistle win.

  24. Pelicans (3–18)

    Efficiency holes + schedule teeth; must lift rim deterrence to stop the slide.

  25. Bulls (9–10)

    Bench minutes keep them competitive; shot diet consistency is the lever.

  26. Celtics (11–9)

    Talent base says patience; glass and crunch-time organization decide trajectory.

  27. Hornets (6–14)

    When LaMelo runs, they score; defensive floor still searching.

  28. Pacers (4–16)

    Pace without stops is empty calories; retool the shell, then run.

  29. Nets (3–16)

    Creating shots isn’t the problem; preventing them is. Bottom-tier defensive metrics to date.

  30. Wizards (2–16)

    Need a defensive baseline first; everything else flows from that.

  • Victor Wembanyama Spurs
